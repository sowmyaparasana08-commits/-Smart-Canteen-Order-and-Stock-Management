## Case Study: Smart Canteen Order and Stock Management

### Problem Statement
Process a sequence of customer orders at a canteen, compute itemized and total bills, deduct stock for valid requests, reject items with insufficient stock or invalid quantities/codes, and produce a daily sales and remaining inventory summary.

### Key Features Implemented
* **Order & Stock Validation:** Checks item availability, positive integer quantities, and remaining stock before deducting inventory.
* **Bill Calculation:** Computes line totals, order subtotals, tax, and net bills for fulfilled items while reporting rejected items clearly.
* **Daily & Stock Reporting:** Summarizes order outcomes, total revenue, units sold per item, and remaining stock at the end of the day.
